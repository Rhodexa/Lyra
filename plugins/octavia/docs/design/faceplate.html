Octavia · faceplate DRAFT 2

The instrument page Octavia shows inside Lyra: a synth made to be cheap. Drawn from colors.css and the brief in docs/FACEPLATE.md. Press Play: the LCD is driven by a real render (the engine's decisions, ringing strings and curves, plus its sound), the same data the telemetry will carry. Touch a control and its value shows on the LCD.

Settled

  • The vibe: blocky and flat, square corners, hard shadows, few decorations; slightly off, never polished.
  • LCD colours: --lcd-0…3 (the lavender set).
  • The LCD cheats: plain text with LCD shadows (the colours and shadows sell it, not segment shapes); segments only for the take. Unlit things are off or barely there, never half-lit.
  • Graphs are smooth vector lines; meters are BIOS-style outlined boxes with blocks.
  • Knobs round, with an arc. Logo: later, probably a raster.
