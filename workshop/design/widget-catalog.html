Palette

Two levels only: background and surface, both neutral charcoal. Edges are darker than what they edge. Text is soft white, never mint; mint and the other lights are only for lights (LEDs, lit keys, knob arcs, fader fills, the playhead). Click a swatch to copy its hex.

Knobs

Four looks chosen by drawn size: Tiny (flat disc, under 34 px), Rubber (ringed cap, under 62 px), Polymer (checker-lit body, larger) and Pan (bipolar under 44 px). 270° sweep. Choice params (2–12 options) show dots instead of an arc.

App size (knob_small 28, 44, knob_large 72, pan 28)

The arc is the channel's light (mid tone); the mixer's pan uses MINT. Hover lifts the body 8 %. In the app, values show on hover/drag, not under every knob.

Silicone keys

Seen straight from the top, lit from above: a thin dark recess, a bezel pad (brighter top, constant middle, darker bottom) and a flat face. Lit, the face glows in its own light and the pad takes the light's rim. Zero gloss. In the app the big keys now belong to Fluttersine and the step grid uses the flat key.

LEDs

A round recessed light that toggles (mute = lit when audible, on/off). Lit, its well takes its own light's colour. Rows of LEDs can be swept: press one and drag over its neighbours.

LCD & dropdown

A pale backlit panel with dark ink. Hover a digit to choose how fine, drag or wheel, double-click to type (the BPM). Dropdowns sit in a recessed well.

CURRENT · IN APP

Faders

A recessed track lit from the bottom up to a three-layer cap with grip lines. The track itself is the indicator (no second value bar).

Meters

Linear in dB from −60 to 0 dBFS. The colour is fixed to the scale (green, lime, amber, rose at the top); the level only reveals it. A pale peak line waits 0.4 s, then falls.

Cleanup notes

Things found while matching this page to the code.
