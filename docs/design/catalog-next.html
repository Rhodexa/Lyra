The simplified widget set, designed here before anything changes in the app. Everything below is built only from the foundations. Nothing is approved yet; every number is there to be argued with. The app as it is today: widget-catalog.html. Not for Fluttersine or Chrysalis.

Foundations

Widgets ask for a role (a control's body, a well, dim ink), never a colour. Today's app has about 40 greys typed inside widgets; these 13 roles plus the light replace them.

Button UI-NEEDS 1

Draft 2. Sizes 28 · 40 · 52 (28 is the smallest anything may be). Built like a small silicone key: dark recess, a pad lit from above, a face; pressed, the light comes from below. Legends always in caps, semibold, letter-spaced. On lights the face like a lit key; soft is a dimmer lit face for when the bright one glares. Pick one.

Draft 1 vs draft 2, at 40 px

Draft 1 was a flat fill with a hairline: a web button, not Lyra's material. Draft 2 is built like the silicone keys and knobs (recess, a pad lit from above, a face), with a bigger, caps, semibold legend and softer corners.
