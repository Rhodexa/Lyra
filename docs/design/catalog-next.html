Foundations

Widgets ask for a role (a control's body, a well, dim ink), never a colour. Today's app has about 40 greys typed inside widgets; these 13 roles plus the light replace them.

Button UI-NEEDS 1

Draft 5, from your tuning: a flat plate (no gradient, ever), a 2 px outline in the button's own colour, darkened 50 %. Disabled buttons have no outline, so a grey plate gets a dark grey line and a lit green one a dark green line, like vector art. Corners 4 px (fine up to 6). Roboto Condensed, semibold, caps. "On" is backlit: the body lights up and the legend turns dark, like a stencil in front of a light (settled; the tuner keeps "backlit + bright outline" and "deep" as ideas for themes). The tuner still works; Copy gives me the numbers.

Mode switch UI-NEEDS 2 · SETTLED

One choice among a few: a radio group, joined into one object so it reads as "pick one" before you click. Two options toggle when you click either one (SONG / PAT, as you like it); three or more behave as a radio. The chosen option is backlit, like a button that's on. Tabs UI-NEEDS 3 · SETTLED

A tab row belongs to the panel under it: switching tabs changes the whole panel, not a setting. Equal cells across the panel, caps labels that clip with an ellipsis instead of colliding with the edges. The rule (Rhodexa): what changes the whole panel sits outside it; what changes a setting sits inside. Settled: T2, folder tabs, packed (neighbours share one outline, 4 px inside), equal cells, ellipsis. Modern, with a touch of old X Windows. T3, the track above something, is kept for switching content that isn't a panel, if we ever need it. T1, the track inside the panel, is retired. Click the tabs. The panels are the real ones: the left panel (300 px wide, like the app's) and the audio-clip editor.

Dropdown UI-NEEDS 4 · SETTLED: D3

Today's area picker, on the new foundations: the 2 px outline in its own colour, the label font in caps, S · M · L. D1 · well: a sunken well, like the track (it shows a value). D2 · plate: a button with a chevron (it reads as "press me"). The popup is a panel of rows; the chosen row is either lit (backlit, like "on") or sel (the foundations' selected: lifted, with a lit edge). Settled: D3 · well + LCD drawer (Rhodexa's idea): closed it's the dark well; open, the drawer is the opposite colour, the LCD's pale panel with dark ink, the chosen row slightly grey, so the list you're reading can't blend into the UI behind it. The first three are drawn open; the rest open on click.
