Foundations

Widgets ask for a role (a control's body, a well, dim ink), never a colour. Today's app has about 40 greys typed inside widgets; these 13 roles plus the light replace them.

Draft 5, from your tuning: a flat plate (no gradient, ever), a 2 px outline in the button's own colour, darkened 50 %. "On" is backlit: the body lights up and the legend turns dark, like a stencil in front of a light (settled; the tuner keeps "backlit + bright outline" and "deep" as ideas for themes). Tabs UI-NEEDS 3 · SETTLED

A tab row belongs to the panel under it: switching tabs changes the whole panel, not a setting. Equal cells across the panel, caps labels that clip with an ellipsis instead of colliding with the edges. The rule (Rhodexa): what changes the whole panel sits outside it; what changes a setting sits inside. Settled: T2, folder tabs, packed (neighbours share one outline, 4 px inside), equal cells, ellipsis. Modern, with a touch of old X Windows. T3, the track above something, is kept for switching content that isn't a panel, if we ever need it. T1, the track inside the panel, is retired. Click the tabs. The panels are the real ones: the left panel (300 px wide, like the app's) and the audio-clip editor.

Dropdown UI-NEEDS 4 · SETTLED: D3

Today's area picker, on the new foundations: the 2 px outline in its own colour, the label font in caps, S · M · L. D1 · well: a sunken well, like the track (it shows a value). D2 · plate: a button with a chevron (it reads as "press me"). The popup is a panel of rows; the chosen row is either lit (backlit, like "on") or sel (the foundations' selected: lifted, with a lit edge). Settled: D3 · well + LCD drawer (Rhodexa's idea): closed it's the dark well; open, the drawer is the opposite colour, the LCD's pale panel with dark ink, the chosen row slightly grey, so the list you're reading can't blend into the UI behind it. The first three are drawn open; the rest open on click.

Number picker UI-NEEDS 5 · SETTLED: USABLE AS IS

The BPM display's behaviour as a widget, for tempo, swing, gain, pitch, lengths. Hover a digit: it's marked, and drag up / down or wheel change that place (tens, ones, tenths…); the drag keeps the raw value and only the display snaps. Double-click to type (Enter keeps, Esc cancels). Draft 2: every digit is its own bigger cell (easier to aim at), less padding around the panel; hovering the blank part or the unit aims at the ones; at L the unit is a tag: a dark label inside the screen's bottom-left corner (square where it meets the edges, rounded at its free corner). Draft 3: the LCD is dim by default; its outline is the ink colour, with an inner shadow, so the screen sits in the panel. Two looks: NP1 · LCD (the pale panel: "a number you can change", like today's BPM) and NP2 · well (dark, like the closed dropdown).

Busy · drop target UI-NEEDS 25 · 26 · DRAFT 1

Busy: never block, never spin. A known length gets a progress track (the slider's track, lit to the progress) with a quiet percentage and a bare Cancel; an unknown length gets a short lit segment sliding back and forth. It lives in the context bar with a pulsing activity LED; the app stays usable. On the thing itself: a loading list row gets a thin line along its bottom; a clip being processed gets a slow light sweep. Drop: show the result. Drag a sample over a lane and a ghost clip appears at the snapped spot, at its real length; places that take things but aren't timelines fill with a soft tint and a corner tag says what will happen. While you drag, every valid place shows a faint tint; invalid ones do nothing.
