The simplified widget set, designed here before anything changes in the app. Everything below is built only from the foundations. Nothing is approved yet; every number is there to be argued with. The app as it is today: widget-catalog.html. Not for Fluttersine or Chrysalis.

Foundations

Widgets ask for a role (a control's body, a well, dim ink), never a colour. Today's app has about 40 greys typed inside widgets; these 13 roles plus the light replace them.

Colour roles

States: one look each, for every widget

statehow it looksbutton, as an example
idlea flat plate lighter than the surface, a 2 px outline in its own colour at half brightness
hoverplate a step lighter, white legend
pressedplate a step darker
ona deep tone of the light (outline: the same, darker), legend in the light's brightest tone
selectedthe surface lifts to surface-sel, plus a 2 px line in its light (strips, rows, tiles; not controls)
disabledplate sinks toward the surface, faint ink, no hover
playingflashes its light with the music (rack name keys, clips under the playhead)see list row, later
focusnothing: no rings, no glows
